Human-AI Ecosystem (HAE!) Lab.

Hey there! 👋 Welcome to the Human-AI Ecosystem (HAE!) Lab.

We are a group of researchers, designers, and developers curious about how AI is reshaping our social interactions and relationships. We study questions such as: How do people seek and adopt help from AI to navigate social scenarios? How do people perceive and respond socially to AI agents? And how does AI mediate the dynamics of human teams, groups, and broader social ecosystems?

We approach these questions through three complementary lenses: understanding human behavior, experimenting with alternative AI designs, and rethinking the policies and sociotechnical infrastructures that shape how human-AI interactions unfold.

Ultimately, we envision a future where AI reshapes, yet integrates seamlessly into, our human ecosystems... hopefully making them better ones.

Research

We research, design, and build AI-powered technologies that account for humans' complex social dynamics and empower users in their situated ecosystems (e.g., professional domains, healthcare systems, and social networks). Here are some of the key projects that we work on at the moment:
  1. AI-mediated social support and its implications for human social interaction: From conflict resolution and intergenerational communication to relationship advice, people increasingly turn to AI to read between the lines and navigate all sorts of interpersonal situations. We examine whether, when, and how people adopt AI’s suggestions and what downstream impacts those choices have on their real-world social interactions and relationships.
  2. Companion AI that connects users back to their support networks: Companionship is now one of the most common uses of generative AI. How do such AI uses become detrimental, and when do human interventions become necessary? Ultimately, how do we disengage users from harmful reliance on AI and reconnect them to existing human support within their personal and healthcare networks?
  3. AI teammates that support positive group dynamics: AI now holds the agency not only to facilitate but also to participate in teams and groups. How do AI agents steer social interactions in groups and teams? How do we design agents that are more socially aware and support positive dynamics?
  4. Collaborative AI tools that foreground human expertise: AI assistance at work holds unprecedented potential to democratize expertise across domains. How do we design AI tools that empower users while spotlighting the value of human work? How should workflows and AI use policies be framed to support this goal?
